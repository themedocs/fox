Your website security depends on all components: hosting, theme, plugins. Note that the theme is only a part of the entire picture. From our side, we ensure The Fox theme is safe, secured on its own. However, no themes can secure your website. It can only do its part.

Each hosting has its own security configured and recommended plugins to secure their WordPress sites. We highly recommend you to install & configure one or all following plugins if your hosting has no security plugins.

- [Wordfence Security – Firewall, Malware Scan, and Login Security](https://wordpress.org/plugins/wordfence/)
- [All-in-one Security](https://wordpress.org/plugins/all-in-one-wp-security-and-firewall/)
- [Jetpack](https://wordpress.org/plugins/jetpack/) and its security module.

They’re free and have paid versions. But even free versions are much much better than no plugins at all.
