The Fox uses native WordPress Customizer for settings. To back up/export theme settings so that you can restore/import later, you can use

this plugin

.

### To export / back up settings

![](/fox/assets/export-settings.jpg)

Step 1

: Install & activate

Customizer Export/Import plugin

.

Step 2

: Go to

Customizer > Export/Import

> click

Export

. You'll get a *dat file. That's your file containing your theme settings. You can store it somewhere (Google Drive, Dropbox..).

### To import / recover settings

![](/fox/assets/import_settings.jpg)

Step 1

: Install & activate

Customizer Export/Import plugin

.

Step 2

: Go to

Customizer > Export/Import

> upload your *dat file > click

Import

.
