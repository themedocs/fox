Fox comes with possibility to create unlimited number of sidebars. You can create new sidebars in **Dashboard > Fox Magazine > Sidebar Manager**.

![](/fox/assets/image-17.png)

1. Go to *Dashboard > Fox Magazine > Sidebar Manager*.
2. Enter sidebar name. Sidebar slug is optional.
3. Hit “Add sidebar” button. You’re done.
4. Now go to *Appearance > Widgets*, you’ll see your **My Sidebar** sidebar appears there.

![](/fox/assets/image-18.png)
