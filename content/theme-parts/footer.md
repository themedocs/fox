### Understand the Concept:

Despite of which demo you are using, The Fox comes with 2 footer areas called “**Footer Sidebar**” and “**Footer Bottom**”. In certain demo, you may see it being enabled or disabled. But you can always enable or disable each section as you want.

To customize your site Footer, please go to **Customize > Footer** panel.

![](/fox/assets/image-14.png)

![](/fox/assets/image-15.png)

### Footer Sidebar

![](/fox/assets/image-16.png)

Footer Sidebar is enabled by default. If the demo you use doesn’t have it, It means it’s been disabled. To enable / disable it, please go to *Customize > Footer > Footer Sidebar*.

Footer Sidebar area includes 4 widget areas: Footer 1 – Footer 4. To add / remove / edit elements from those widgets, there’re 2 ways:

- Go to *Customize > Widgets > Footer j* for any j from 1 to 4, you’ll see widgets there of the sidebar “Footer j”.
- Go to *Dashboard > Appearance > Widgets*, you’ll see all sidebar there. Find widgets Footer 1-4

### Footer Bottom

Footer Bottom is enabled by default. If the demo you use doesn’t have it, It means it’s been disabled. To enable / disable it, please go to *Customize > Footer > Footer Bottom*.
