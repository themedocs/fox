### Other hosting options

If you trust wordpress.com more, you can go with [wordpress.com business plan](https://wordpress.com/plans/) ($25/month if you pay annually).

For budget hosting, [SiteGround](https://www.siteground.com/?referrer_id=9374487) is also a good option. Its first year is heavily discounted and the price goes up at renewal, so check the current rate for both before you commit.

For other hosting options, I recommend using [WP Rocket](https://wp-rocket.me/) + [Imagify](https://imagify.io/) to optimize your site.
