#### How to get refund?

Please [open a refund request here](https://themeforest.net/refund_requests/new). Envato asks you to pick the item and a reason; the request reaches us, and we approve it if the purchase is within 14 days. If It's more than 14 days, unfortunately we cannot accept the request.
