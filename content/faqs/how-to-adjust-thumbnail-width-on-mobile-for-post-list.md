title: "How to adjust thumbnail width on mobile for Post List"
description: "If your post list thumbnail on mobile is too small you can go to Customize > Blog/Archive > Thumbnail > Post list: Thumbnail width to adjust it."
---

If your post list thumbnail on mobile is too small you can go to **Customize > Blog/Archive > Thumbnail > Post list: Thumbnail width** to adjust it.

This applies to Archive post list like Category page, Author page.. In case you need to adjust post list thumbnail width on mobile, you go to **Customize > Homepage Builder > the section > Elements tab > scroll to Thumbnail area**.

![](/fox/assets/image-62-1024x643-1.jpg)

There’s another solution, is to set post list layout to be stack on mobile, ie. Thumbnail above and text below.

For archive pages, you go to **Customize > Blog/Archive > Post Item > [LIST] Mobile layout**
