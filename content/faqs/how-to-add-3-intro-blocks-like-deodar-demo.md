---
title: "How to add 3 intro blocks like Deodar demo?"
description: ""
---

![](/fox/assets/image-1-1024x364-1.png)

**Step 1**: Create a custom sidebar with any name, eg. “My Sidebar 1”. Check [this article](/fox/theme-parts/sidebar-widgets/) for creating a custom sidebar.

**Step 2**: Go to *Customize > Homepage Builder > Add Section*.

**Step 3**: In your section, choose layout “Custom sidebar”with layout “3 columns”

![](/fox/assets/image-2-380x1024-1.png)

**Step 4**: Go to *Dashboard > Appearance > Widgets* to drop 3 “(FOX) Imagebox” widgets into your “My Sidebar 1” area.

![](/fox/assets/image-3-1024x386-1.png)
