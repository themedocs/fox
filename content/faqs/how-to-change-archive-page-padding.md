To change archive page padding, please change its titlebar. You can search for “Titlebar” in the Customizer search bar, It’ll take you to the right place to change title bar padding.

![](/fox/assets/image-5-477x1024-1.png)

Or you can go directly to *Customize > Blog/Archive > Titlebar*, you’ll find the padding option there.

![](/fox/assets/image-6-435x1024-1.png)
