If your YouTube video in your content doesn’t fit post width or It looks ugly, unequal.. The reason can be some bad lazy load plugin.

![](/fox/assets/image-65-1024x503-1.jpg)

If you use EWWW Image Optimizer plugin, please go to *Dashboard > Settings > EWWW Image Optimizer* to turn of Lazy load feature.

If you use other plugins for optimization, performance or sort of, please find corresponding setting for Lazy load and turn it off.
