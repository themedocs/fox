A featured post is a post marked as “star” in your post list. It’s like an “Editor-chosen” post. With featured post system, you can display Editor-pick posts only in your homepage or in the widget.

![](/fox/assets/image-66-1024x399-1.jpg)

You can also set or unset a featured post in single post settings.

![](/fox/assets/image-67-1024x648-1.jpg)
