---
title: "How to customize footer pop-up posts?"
description: "The footer popup posts for single posts when you scroll down to bottom are called “Footer Side Dock“."
---

The footer popup posts for single posts when you scroll down to bottom are called “**Footer Side Dock**“.

Please go to **Customize > Single Post > Footer Side Dock** to enable/disable or customize it.
